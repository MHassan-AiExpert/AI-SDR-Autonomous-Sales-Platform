# F32 — Midnight & Brass Interface: Use Cases

Engineered by Muhammad Hassan | AI ENGINEER | AUTOMATION SPECIALIST | SOFTWARE ENGINEER | FULL STACK DEVELOPER

## Actors

| Actor | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Any signed-in person | Owner, admin, rep or viewer using a workspace |
| Signed-out visitor | Someone on the landing, sign-in or sign-up page |
| Agency | Runs client workspaces under its own brand colour |

## UC-F32-01 Choose dark or light

1. The person presses **Dark** or **Light** at the bottom of the sidebar.
2. The choice is saved for a year and the same page reloads in the new theme.
3. Every later page opens in that theme straight away, with no flash.

**Alternative:** someone who never chooses gets their device's setting.

## UC-F32-02 Find a page

1. The person scans the sidebar groups: Sell, Outreach, Insights or Manage.
2. They open a page. It stays highlighted, and so do pages below it (for example a single lead under Leads).

## UC-F32-03 Act on what needs attention

1. Home shows, for example, "2 Conversations waiting for a person", "38 Emails to approve" and "1 Alert to check".
2. Each card has one button that opens the page that already handles it: open handoffs, campaigns, or warnings.
3. When nothing is waiting, Home says "You are all caught up".

## UC-F32-04 Search leads from anywhere

1. The person types a name, company or email into the top bar.
2. The Leads page opens, filtered by that text.

## UC-F32-05 Agency brand colour

1. An agency sets its colour, for example a pale yellow or a deep green.
2. Buttons, highlights, links and focus rings use that colour, and button text switches to dark or white so it stays readable.

## UC-F32-06 Use on a phone

1. The sidebar sits above the page and its links scroll sideways in one row.
2. Nothing on the page needs sideways scrolling.
